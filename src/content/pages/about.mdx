# ABOUT GRAHAM COUNTY MESH

Graham County Mesh is a community project focused on **Meshtastic, practical radio skills, and resilient local communication** in western North Carolina.

This isn't a commercial network.

It's a place to learn, experiment, build coverage, and share what works.

## Why Meshtastic?

Meshtastic uses inexpensive LoRa radios to create a decentralized mesh. Nodes can relay messages for one another, allowing communication beyond the range of a single radio.

That makes it interesting for:

- Outdoor recreation
- Emergency preparedness
- Events
- Backcountry communication
- Neighborhood communication
- Learning RF and embedded technology

## What we value

**Useful over flashy.**  
Build things that work.

**Local over generic.**  
A mountain network has different problems than a city network.

**Open knowledge.**  
Share measurements, mistakes, builds, and lessons.

**Privacy matters.**  
A mesh is not automatically private or anonymous. Understand what your device is transmitting before relying on it for sensitive information.

## Get involved

The best network is the one people actually use.

Bring a node. Test a route. Host a repeater. Share a build. Tell us where the coverage fails.
