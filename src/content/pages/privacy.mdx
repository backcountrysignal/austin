# PRIVACY

Graham County Mesh is designed to publish practical information about a community radio project without unnecessarily publishing personal information.

Do not submit private, sensitive, or identifying information to public channels or forms.

Node locations may be generalized or withheld when publishing an exact location could create a privacy or security concern.

This website may use standard server logs and hosting telemetry provided by its hosting platform. As the project adds forms, analytics, or other integrations, this page will be updated to describe them.
